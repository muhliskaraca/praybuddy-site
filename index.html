Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Pray Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

Pray Buddy is built with privacy at its core. We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data (GPS is used locally on your device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction — it is never sent to any server)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial information

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and is never transmitted:

  • Your prayer tracking history (weekly grid)
  • App settings (language, calculation method, age group, voice preferences)
  • User profiles (name and preferences)
  • Dhikr counter progress

Location Services

Pray Buddy uses your device's location to:

  • Calculate accurate prayer times for your position
  • Determine the Qibla (direction of the Kaaba in Mecca)
  • Display your city name on the home screen

Your location is processed entirely on your device. It is never sent to our servers or any third party. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Pray Buddy schedules local notifications on your device for prayer time reminders. These notifications are managed entirely by iOS and do not involve any external server.

Third-Party Services

Pray Buddy does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain ads.

When using the Diyanet/DITIB prayer time source, the app connects to the Diyanet API to fetch prayer times for your selected district. Only the district ID is sent — no personal data.

Children's Privacy

Pray Buddy includes a Kids Mode for children aged 6-12. The app does not collect any data from children. All features work entirely offline and on-device.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all Pray Buddy data by uninstalling the app. No server-side data exists to delete.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

FAQ

Q: Why are my prayer times different from my mosque?

A: Prayer times vary by calculation method. Try switching to a different method in Settings (e.g., Diyanet/DITIB for Turkish mosques, Muslim World League for international).

Q: How do I change the app language?

A: Go to Settings → Appearance → Language. Choose from English, Turkish, Arabic, Spanish, or French.

Q: Is the Hijri date accurate?

A: Pray Buddy uses the Umm al-Qura calendar (Saudi standard). Dates may vary by ±1 day depending on your region's moon sighting authority.

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes — prayer times via GPS, Qibla compass, Dhikr, Duas, and the Tracker all work without internet. Only Diyanet prayer times require a brief connection.
