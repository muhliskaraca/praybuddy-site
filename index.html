Pray Buddy

Your companion for the five daily prayers – accurate times, Qibla, tracking and gentle reminders. Private and fully on-device.

What it does

Everything for your five daily prayers.

Accurate prayer times

Calculated on your device with the open-source adhan-swift engine – or via Diyanet times if you prefer. Works fully offline.

Qibla compass

Find the direction of the Kaaba using your device's magnetometer and location, computed on-device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Prayer tracking

Mark each prayer as done and keep a private record. All tracking stays on your device – no account, no cloud.

Du'as with translation

Everyday supplications shown in Arabic with transliteration and translation, so you can read and learn along.

Home Screen widgets

The date, your location and the next prayer at a glance. Widgets read only local data.

Gentle reminder voices

Pre-installed boy, girl and adult voices remind you and cheer you on after a completed prayer – bundled with the app, played locally.

Two modes

Made for the whole family.

Kids Mode

An age-appropriate experience with encouraging voices and warm confirmations, built to make prayer feel welcoming rather than a duty. No data is collected in Kids Mode.

Adult Mode

A calmer, grown-up tone with adult reminder voices and quiet confirmations after each prayer. Same privacy, same on-device approach.

Privacy by design

Private. On your device. No exceptions.

Pray Buddy has no account, no cloud sync, no trackers, no analytics and no ads. Prayer times and Qibla are computed on your iPhone; your location is used only while the app is open and is never stored or shared. The full details are in the privacy policy below.

  • No account, no sign-up
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads
  • Everything stored locally – delete the app to remove all data

2. What data does Pray Buddy collect?

Pray Buddy is built with data minimization as a core principle. The app only collects and processes:

  • Location data (only during active use) to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction
  • Local app settings (prayer method, language, volume) – stored only on your device
  • Prayer tracking data – stored only locally on your device

3. No third-party sharing

No personal data is shared with, sold to, or used by third parties for advertising purposes. There are no trackers, no analytics, and no ads in the app.

4. Location data

The app uses your location solely to calculate local prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never stored, transmitted, or shared with third parties. Location access is only requested during active use of the app.

5. Local data storage

All user data (settings, prayer tracking, profiles) is stored exclusively on your device. There is no cloud sync and no server upload.

6. Audio content

The app contains pre-installed prayer reminder voices (boy, girl and adult variants for Kids Mode and Adult Mode, plus confirmation sounds after completed prayers). All audio is bundled with the app and plays locally. Pray Buddy does not include a classical Adhan call, and there are no audio recordings of Du'as (Du'as are shown as text with Arabic, transliteration and translation).

7. Prayer time calculation

Pray Buddy calculates prayer times in two ways, depending on your settings:

  • On-device calculation via the open-source adhan-swift library. No data leaves the device.
  • Optional: Diyanet (Presidency of Religious Affairs of Turkey) prayer times via the public endpoint ezanvakti.emushaf.net, if you explicitly select a Diyanet location during onboarding. Only the district identifier is transmitted, no personal data or device information.

8. Qibla direction and widgets

The Qibla compass uses your device's magnetometer and your current location on-device. The Home Screen widgets (date, location and upcoming prayer) also read only local data and do not send anything to a server.

9. User rights

Since no personal data is stored on our servers, the usual rights of access and deletion do not apply. All data can be completely removed by deleting the app.

10. Children (Kids Mode)

Pray Buddy includes a Kids Mode with age-appropriate content. No personal data is collected or transmitted in Kids Mode either.
